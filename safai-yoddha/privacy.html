Safai Yoddha – Privacy Policy

Effective 24 November 2026

In short: Safai Yoddha is a game that teaches children good habits. We do not ask for a name, e-mail, phone number or account. The game saves only on your phone. Ads appear only when the player taps a button to watch one for a reward; they are shown by Google AdMob, are never personalised, and are child-safe for children.

Who we are

Safai Yoddha is made by an independent developer. Contact: developer.pht@gmail.com.

What the game keeps on your phone

The game saves your progress (days played, stars, points, the Yoddha you chose, language and sound settings, the age band you picked) in the app's private storage on your phone. It is never sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game deletes it.

The age band (5–7, 8–10, 11–13, 14–17, 18+) is asked only to choose the Yoddha's look and to set the ads correctly (see below). It stays on the phone.

What we collect

Nothing. We (the developer) do not collect, receive or store any personal information. The game has no accounts, no chat, no sharing with other players, and no analytics of our own. It does not use the camera, microphone, contacts, location or files.

Ads

The game is complete without ads. In four places a player may choose to watch a short rewarded ad: to get a power (Shakti), to double the day's stars, to show where the day's stars are hidden, or to fly to an emergency call. There are never pop-up, banner or forced ads, and at most three ads a day.

These ads are shown by Google AdMob, a Google Play Families self-certified ads SDK, and are always requested as non-personalised:

Age band picked in the gameHow ads are requested
5–13, or no age picked yetChild-directed (COPPA), content rated G, never personalised
14–17Under the age of consent, content rated G, never personalised
18+Never personalised, content rated PG; in the EEA/UK Google's consent form is shown where the law requires it

The game does not use the Android Advertising ID (the permission is removed from the app). To show an ad, Google's SDK receives technical information such as the IP address (from which an approximate location may be derived), device and app information, an app-set identifier, and whether an ad was viewed or tapped, and may use it for ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and diagnostics. Google's handling of this data is described in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Internet

The game plays fully offline. It uses the internet only to load an ad when the player asks for one.

Children

The game is made for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. Ads for children are child-directed and never personalised. Parents can play without ads simply by not tapping the ad buttons.

Your choices

  • Don't tap an ad button: no ad is requested.
  • Delete the game's data: Android Settings → Apps → Safai Yoddha → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the game.
  • Adults in the EEA/UK can review ad consent in Google's form when it is shown.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions or requests, including from parents: developer.pht@gmail.com.
