Sabji Mandi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 November 2026. Applies to the Sabji Mandi game for Android (com.mohitsharma.sabjimandi) and its desktop and web versions.

In short

Sabji Mandi has no accounts and no analytics, and your game progress is saved on your own device (and, if your phone backs up apps, in your own Google backup). The game shows an ad only when you choose to watch one for a bonus (rewarded ads); those ads come from Google AdMob, which collects some data as described below.

What stays on your device

  • Your save (days played, money, upgrades, farm, settings) is stored in the app's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the app deletes it from the phone.
  • Phone backup. If Android backup is on for your Google account, Android copies this one save file into your own backup (and to a new phone when you move), so your game carries over. It is stored by Google under your account, encrypted, and the game's maker cannot see it. Turn it off in your phone's Settings → Google → Backup. Nothing else from the game is backed up.
  • Tester code. If you type a tester code in Settings, it is kept in your save so the parts it opens stay open. The game checks it on the phone; it is never sent anywhere.
  • Crash reports. If the game crashes, a short text report (game version, what screen you were on, and the error) is written to the app's private storage. It is never sent anywhere.
  • Optional play events. Settings has an option, off by default, that keeps a small log of gameplay events (for example "day 3 finished") on the device. The game does not send this log anywhere.

Ads (Google AdMob)

If you tap "Ad dekho", the ad is served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google's SDK collects your device's advertising ID, approximate location from your IP address, and information about your device and how you interact with the ad. Google's use of this data is described at How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. The game asks AdMob for ads suitable for all ages only.

In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the game first shows Google's consent form; you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, on most Android phones).

Playing together (Saath-Saath)

Co-op is not in the version on Google Play yet; this section applies once it is. When you choose to play co-op with a friend, your phone connects over an encrypted connection (wss://) to our relay server, which passes game moves between the players in a room. The relay sees your device's IP address (as any internet connection does), the room code, the preset name and avatar you picked in the game, and a random rejoin code it hands your phone for that room. The relay keeps this only in memory while the room is open and does not store or log it afterwards. The game itself sends no install or advertising identifier there, and there is no chat or free text. Single-player games never connect to the relay.

Permissions

  • Internet: for the ads you choose to watch (and co-op games, once available), as above.
  • Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob for the ads, as above.
  • Vibration: short buzzes for game feedback (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Notifications: at most one reminder an evening about what waits in your game, made on the phone itself; nothing is sent anywhere (turn off in Settings → Reminders).

Children

The game is made for a general audience. It does not knowingly collect information from children itself, and it asks AdMob for family-safe ads only.
